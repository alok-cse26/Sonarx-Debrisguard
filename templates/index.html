Technology/Service: AI-Powered Underwater Marine-Debris & Anomaly Detection Using Side-Scan Sonar Imagery.

1. Technology Snapshot & SDG Connection

Problem: The seabed is the final resting place of a large share of marine litter – lost and abandoned fishing gear (ghost nets), plastics, tyres, metal drums, cables and wreckage. Satellites cannot see it, and cameras fail in deep, dark or turbid water.

What our technology solves: AI models automatically detect and classify marine debris in SSS imagery, and an anomaly-detection layer flags unusual or previously unseen objects. Raw sonar scans become geo-referenced debris and hazard maps that guide targeted clean-up.

Strongest SDG Connection: SDG 14 (Life Below Water)
Target 14.1 asks for preventing and significantly reducing marine pollution, including marine debris. Debris that cannot be located cannot be removed.
3 & 4. Impact Detective & Fix The Technology
🟢 Positive Impact: Faster, consistent seabed screening (minutes instead of weeks). Low-cost SSS + edge AI makes monitoring affordable for smaller agencies.
🔴 Negative Impact: Energy and carbon footprint of training heavy deep-learning models, plus fuel burned by survey vessels.
🟡 Unexpected Impact: Not all debris is harmful: old wrecks, tyres and hard objects can become artificial habitat. Careless removal based on detections alone could destroy that habitat!
💡 One-Line Verdict: "Our technology contributes to cleaner oceans and safer, better-monitored seabeds (SDG 14), but its sustainability challenge is the energy cost of deep-learning models and false detections caused by noisy sonar and limited labelled data. We propose a lightweight, edge-deployable, human-in-the-loop detector that combines supervised detection with anomaly detection, transfer learning and explainable outputs."
