TECHNOLOGY BOTTLENECK

Why Optical Cameras Fail Underwater

❌ Optical ROVs & Diver Teams (Status Quo)

  • Turbidity Barrier: River silt & monsoons reduce visibility to <1.5 meters.
  • Prohibitive Cost: Chartering survey vessels & divers costs $15,000–$25,000 / day.
  • Minimal Swath: Covers less than 0.1 km² per day.
  • Human Danger: High-risk decompression diving in harbor fairways.

✅ SonarX Acoustic Side-Scan (Our Solution)

  • Zero-Visibility Penetration: 455 kHz sound waves ignore muddy silt.
  • Expansive Coverage: 150-meter swath covers 6.5+ km² per sortie.
  • Autonomous Electric AUV: Subsea execution for under $2,200 / day (85% cheaper).
  • Real-Time Edge INT8: Instant target geo-tagging at 74 frames/sec.
🎙️ Speaker Notes: Optical cameras are useless in turbid coastal waters like the Bay of Bengal where visibility is under 5 feet. High-frequency side-scan sonar is the only physics-based modality that works, piercing turbid water with a 150m swath.
SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

The 6-Stage AI & Digital Signal Processing Pipeline

STAGE 1: DSP

Acoustic Preprocessing

Slant-range rectification eliminates blind nadir zones; logarithmic TVG gain normalizes backscatter intensity; adaptive Lee filtering clears speckle.

STAGE 2: SUPERVISED

YOLOv8-SSS Neural Net

Custom-trained acoustic backbone detects ghost nets, plastic rafts, chemical drums, and tyres at 94.2% mAP@0.5.

STAGE 3: ANOMALY

PatchCore Memory Bank

Zero-shot acoustic anomaly detection flags novel uncataloged hazards (mines, pipeline ruptures) with 97.1% AUROC.

STAGE 4: SYNTHESIS

Synthetic Augmentation

Acoustic diffusion model provides 100× data efficiency, saving $350,000 in expensive offshore sea trials.

STAGE 5: GIS & SATELLITE

12-Ocean Bathymetry

Space-to-seafloor sensor fusion connects Sentinel-1 SAR surface slicks with AUV seabed targets via 3D Lagrangian drift modeling.

STAGE 6: HITL

Human-in-the-Loop

Grad-CAM acoustic explainability heatmaps for port hydrographers + built-in Artificial Reef Habitat Safeguard.

INT8 TensorRT Quantization Feat

By quantizing 32-bit floating point weights down to calibrated 8-bit integers (INT8), model footprint was compressed by 75% with only a 0.9% variance in detection accuracy. The system runs fully embedded on an Nvidia Jetson Orin Nano/NX within standard 24V AUV battery envelopes.

🎙️ Speaker Notes: On an underwater vehicle, power is life. By optimizing through TensorRT INT8 quantization, we achieve 74 frames per second at 13.5 milliseconds while drawing less than 10 watts of power.
