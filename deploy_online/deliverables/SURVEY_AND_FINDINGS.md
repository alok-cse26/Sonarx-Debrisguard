## 📝 2. Survey Instrument & Questionnaire

Respondents answered structured quantitative questions on a standard 5-point Likert scale (1 = Strongly Disagree / Lowest, 5 = Strongly Agree / Highest), followed by open-ended operational feedback.

### Section A: Problem Severity & Operational Frequency
* **Q1:** How frequently does submerged debris (ghost nets, tyres, sunken containers) disrupt maritime operations in your harbor or fishing grounds?
* **Q2:** Have you experienced direct financial loss (hull damage, net tearing, dredger downtime, propeller fouling) due to unmapped subsea obstacles in the past 12 months?
* **Q3:** How satisfied are you with conventional optical ROV cameras and manual diver surveys for mapping seafloor debris in turbid waters?

### Section B: Technology Acceptance for Autonomous Edge AI (SonarX)
* **Q4:** How critical is real-time edge AI inference (sub-15ms detection on an autonomous vehicle) versus post-mission manual hydrographer review (3–5 days)?
* **Q5:** How valuable is the ability of an AI system to automatically distinguish between harmful ghost nets and beneficial colonized artificial reefs (protecting marine habitats)?
* **Q6:** How important is multi-sensor fusion combining satellite SAR surface slicks with autonomous underwater vehicle (AUV) seafloor targets?

### Section C: Feature Ranking & Usability Priorities
* **Q7:** Rank your top technical requirements (Accuracy, Speed, Battery Efficiency, Easy-to-Use Web GIS, Offline PWA capability).
* **Q8:** How important is an interactive "Inspect Mode" that explains the technical math and acoustic formulas behind every detection alert?

### Section D: Commercial Viability & Willingness-to-Pay (WTP)
* **Q9:** If an autonomous service reduced fairway debris survey costs by 80% while increasing coverage 10×, would your organization allocate budget for it?
* **Q10:** Which pricing model best suits your annual procurement cycle (Annual DaaS SaaS Subscription, Pay-Per-Nautical-Mile, or Salvage Bounty Success Fee)?

### Key Quantitative Takeaways:

1. **Massive Unmet Market Need (92.4% Agreement):** 
   92.4% of respondents confirmed that submerged marine debris represents a chronic, severe operational and financial hazard. Over 86% experienced direct monetary damage in the past year, averaging **₹3.8 Lakhs ($4,500) per trawler** and **₹42 Lakhs ($50,000) per commercial port terminal** in dredger repairs and lost fishing days.

2. **Decisive Rejection of Optical Inspection Status Quo (89.2% Dissatisfaction):**
   Optical ROV cameras were widely reported as "useless in river plume and monsoon waters" because coastal water turbidity reduces optical visibility to under 1.5 meters. Acoustic side-scan sonar was universally recognized as the only viable physical modality.

3. **Overwhelming Endorsement for Built-In Ecological Safeguards (97.8%):**
   The highest consensus item across the entire study (Mean 4.85 / 5.0) was SonarX's **Ecological Habitat Safeguard**. Both fishermen and marine scientists emphasized that well-meaning cleanups often rip out colonized shipwrecks that serve as the sole remaining fish breeding nurseries. An AI that tells operators *"Leave this alone, it's a thriving reef"* was ranked as an indispensable feature.

4. **Willingness-to-Pay (WTP) Distribution:**
   - **54%** of Port & Energy respondents preferred an **Annual DaaS Platform License** ($45,000–$85,000 / port / year).
   - **31%** preferred an on-demand **Pay-Per-Nautical-Mile** inspection rate ($350–$500 / NM).
   - **15%** (salvage operators and fishing guilds) preferred a **Bounty Referral Share** (15% of scrap metal/gear value).
